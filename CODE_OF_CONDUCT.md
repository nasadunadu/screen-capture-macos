# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Protect private information found in screenshots, logs, or crash reports.
- Keep discussions relevant to improving the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or abusing the reporting process.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.
